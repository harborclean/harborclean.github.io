Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026 · Last updated: November 26, 2026
In short: Harbor Clean tidies, shrinks and cleans files on your device. The app has no ads, no analytics and no internet access: your files, file names, photos and their details never leave your phone, and we do not collect any personal information through the app.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Harbor Clean Android app (“Harbor Clean”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the Harbor Clean app distributed on Google Play. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

Harbor Clean helps you find and remove files you no longer need: leftover installers, temporary and log files, cached previews, empty folders, large files, duplicate files and recent files. It also lets you browse and manage files (open, share, copy, move, rename, delete and create folders), make large photos smaller, remove hidden details such as location and device model from photos, and shows storage and basic device information (such as CPU, memory and battery status).

  • Only the folders you choose. The app does not ask for permission to access your whole storage. The first time a feature needs files, it asks you to pick a folder in Android’s system folder picker, and it works only inside the folders you picked. You can remove a folder at any time in the app’s Settings.
  • All scanning happens locally. To find files, the app reads file names, sizes, dates and folder structure in those folders, and calculates content fingerprints (hashes) to detect duplicates. This processing happens only on your device.
  • Photo details are read and removed on your device. To show which photos carry hidden details, the app reads the EXIF information stored inside JPEG photos (such as GPS location, camera or phone model and owner name). When you choose to strip it, the app removes those details from the photos you select and keeps the picture itself, its date and its orientation unchanged. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Photo compression happens on your device. When you choose to make photos smaller, the app re-saves the JPEG photos you select and replaces the original only if the new file is noticeably smaller.
  • Nothing is deleted or changed without your confirmation. Files are only removed, compressed or stripped after you select them and confirm.
  • Opening and sharing is up to you. When you open a file with another app or share files through Android’s share sheet, only the files you chose are handed to the app you pick.

4. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through the app. There is no account, and the app does not send your files, file names, file contents, usage statistics, device identifiers or any other data to us or to anyone else.

The app stores a few settings locally on your device, such as the folders you granted, whether reminders are enabled and when you last cleaned. This data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you contact us by email (for example via “Feedback” in the app), we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

5. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or tracking services, and we do not sell, share or transfer any information about you to third parties. When you open or share a file with another app, only the files you chose are handed to the app you pick, and that app’s own privacy policy applies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or some US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because the app does not collect personal information, the only data we may hold about you is a feedback email you chose to send us; to make a request about it, contact us (Section 10).

7. Children’s privacy

Harbor Clean is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and security

Since the content you work with in the app never leaves your device, we do not retain it. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to respond. We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example when we add features. If a future version of the app adds advertising, analytics or any other data collection, we will update this Policy before that version is released. We will post the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or on its Google Play page.
